## 🚀 Completed Tasks  

### **Task 1: Sales Performance Dashboard (Excel)**  
- Imported and cleaned a sales dataset.  
- Built **pivot tables** to summarize revenue, units sold, and monthly trends.  
- Designed a **dashboard with charts** to visualize overall sales performance.  

---

### **Task 2: Exploratory Data Analysis on Titanic Dataset (Python)**  
- Cleaned the Titanic dataset: handled missing values and converted data types.  
- Generated descriptive statistics and insights (e.g., survival by gender/class).  
- Visualized survival patterns using **Matplotlib & Seaborn**.  

---

### **Task 3: Customer Segmentation Using RFM Analysis (Python)**  
- Processed online retail data to calculate **Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM)** metrics.  
- Segmented customers into groups (loyal, at-risk, inactive).  
- Suggested actionable marketing strategies for each segment.  

---

### **Task 4: Data Cleaning & Insight Generation from Survey Data (Python)**  
- Worked with Kaggle Data Science Survey dataset (containing missing values & duplicates).  
- Applied **data cleaning & label encoding** for categorical variables.  
- Extracted insights into respondent preferences and demographics.  

---

### **Task 5: SQL-Based Analysis of Product Sales (SQL)**  
- Queried the **Chinook database** using SQL.  
- Wrote **JOINs & aggregations** to answer key business questions:  
  - Top-selling products  
  - Revenue per region  
  - Monthly sales performance  

---

### **Task 6: Web Scraping & Job Market Analysis (Python)**  
- Scraped job postings from a **static HTML page** saved from RemoteOK.  
- Extracted job titles, companies, locations, posted dates, and skills.  
- Cleaned and structured the dataset using **Pandas**.  
- Performed **skill frequency analysis** to identify in-demand technologies.  
- Visualized results with **bar charts, word clouds, pie charts, and heatmaps**.  
- Provided extra insights such as filtering by skill (Python), skill distribution by location, and company-skill diversity.
